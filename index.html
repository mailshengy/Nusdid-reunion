Games and micro-events

Design Prompt Speed Round

Each table gets the same quirky brief, sketch fast, cheer louder. Gallery walk follows, everyone places a sticker vote.

Tiny Product Sprint

Paper, tape, markers. Build a small thing that solves a funny problem. Judges pick winners for wit, craft, and charm.

Memory Wall

Bring one photo from 2009 to 2013, we print and pin. Add a sticky note with a caption that only your studio would get.

Mini Awards

Sweetest Prototype Fail, Most Unreadable Handwriting, Most Loyal to Pantone 021, and more. Prizes are playful.
